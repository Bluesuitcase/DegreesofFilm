Every film is connected. Prove it.

How it works

  1. Name a person from the starting film — cast or crew, anyone credited.
  2. Name another film they worked on.
  3. Repeat. The chain closes the moment you name someone who also worked on the target film.

Each hop through a person is one degree. Par is the shortest chain that exists — matching it is a good day, beating it means you found something the graph didn't expect. Three wrong links on a single step and the run ends. Misspellings and names nobody's heard of are free; naming a real person who simply isn't in the film costs you.
